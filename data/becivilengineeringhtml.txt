About Department
The Department of Civil Engineering was established in the year 2010 and offers four year Degree Programme in Civil Engineering with an annual intake of 120. The academic activities of the department emphasize deep understanding of fundamental principles, development of creative ability to handle the challenges of Civil Engineering and the analytical ability to solve problems which are interdisciplinary in nature.
The department is committed to the advancement of healthy academic culture and a symbiosis between the teachers and students.
The Department with highly qualified, experienced and dedicated faculty members   engages a mosaic of activities like, industry - institute interaction, research and development, teaching / learning process, conscious development of students leading services to society. Department laboratories adhere to latest equipment and machineries to afford the students with practical knowledge. Computer laboratory at the department provides 24x7 internet connectivity and access to latest softwares.
Industrial Visits and talks by prominent experts in the field, acquaint the students with the recent developments in the Industry. The Department also encourages students to engage in extra-curricular and co-curricular activities essential for nurturing team spirit and organizational skills. The Civil Engineering Association, âFOCUSâ, serves the purpose of providing ample opportunities to the students to showcase their finesse in all aspects. The Association is responsible for activities and events to unfold the prospects of Civil Engineering in the present day world.

PEOs and POs
Programme Educational Objectives (PEOs)
PEO1:
Develop competent Civil Engineering professionals equipped with strong technical knowledge, practical skills, and ethical values to excel in public or private sector organizations and contribute meaningfully to society.
PEO2:
Address real-world infrastructure challenges by functioning as consultants, researchers, or entrepreneurs offering innovative, sustainable, and socially responsible solutions in areas such as housing, water supply, sewerage, transportation, and spatial planning.
PEO3:
Collaborate effectively in multidisciplinary teams and leverage modern tools, technologies, and policies to solve complex engineering problems, support infrastructure development, and promote continuous learning and professional growth.
Programme Outcomes (POs)
Engineering knowledge:
Apply the knowledge of mathematics, science, engineering fundamentals, and an engineering specialization to the solution of complex engineering problems.
Problem analysis:
Identify, formulate, review research literature, and analyze complex engineering problems reaching substantiated conclusions using first principles of Mathematics, natural sciences, and engineering sciences.
Design/development of solutions:
Design solutions for complex engineering problems and design system components or processes that meet the specified needs with appropriate consideration for the public health and safety, and the cultural, societal, and environmental considerations.
Conduct investigations of complex problems:
Use research-based knowledge and research methods including design of experiments, analysis and interpretation of data, and synthesis of the information to provide valid conclusions.
Modern tool usage:
Create, select, and apply appropriate techniques, resources, and modern engineering and IT tools including prediction and modeling to complex engineering activities with an understanding of the limitations.
The engineer and society:
Apply reasoning informed by the contextual knowledge to assess societal, health, safety, legal and cultural issues and the consequent responsibilities relevant to the professional engineering practice.
Environment and sustainability:
Understand the impact of the professional engineering solutions in societal and environmental contexts, and demonstrate the knowledge of, and need for sustainable development.
Ethics:
Apply ethical principles and commit to professional ethics and responsibilities and norms of the engineering practice.
Individual and team work:
Function effectively as an individual, and as a member or leader in diverse teams, and in multidisciplinary settings.
Communication:
Communicate effectively on complex engineering activities with the engineering community and with society at large, such as, being able to comprehend and write effective reports and design documentation, make effective presentations, and give and receive clear instructions.
Project management and finance:
Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of the engineering and management principles and apply these to oneâs own work, as a member and leader in a team, to manage projects and in multidisciplinary environments.
Life-long Learning:
Recognize the need for, and have the preparation and ability to engage in independent and life-long learning in the broadest context of technological change.
Program Specific Objectives (PSOs)
Students will be able to
PSO1:
Demonstrate in-depth knowledge of Civil Engineering discipline, with an ability to evaluate, analyze and synthesize existing and new knowledge.
PSO2:
Critically analyze complex Civil Engineering problems, apply independent judgment for synthesizing information and make innovative advances in a theoretical, practical and policy context.
PSO3:
Conceptualize and solve Civil Engineering problems, evaluate potential solutions and arrive at technically feasible, economically viable and environmentally sound solutions with due consideration of health, safety, and socio-cultural factors.
Scope & Job Oppourtunities
Civil Engineering is considered as one of the oldest infrastructural engineering disciplines and plays a key role:  in public sector, from municipal to national governments, and in private sector, from individual home owners to international companies. There is a wonder combination of academic excellence and industrial experience amongst the faculty, which is instrumental in imparting the right blend of theoretical and practical knowledge to the students.
Civil engineering, the broadest of the engineering field, is considered the most versatile branch devoted to Surveying and Construction Engineering, irrigation engineering, transportation engineering, soils and foundation engineering, geodetic engineering, hydraulic engineering, coastal and ocean engineering, geotechnical engineering, structural engineering, environmental engineering, water resources engineering, materials engineering, control engineering.
Civil Engineers will always be needed to maintain and restructure the existing facilities. Civil Engineering graduates can aspire either for research and teaching or independent consultancy services. In addition, activities like seminars, projects, assignments, quizzes and other such competitions are conducted on regular basis to induce the professional skills in the students, which ultimately help them in getting placed in reputed organizations.
On completion of BE, one can look for jobs in road projects, building work, consultancy firms, quality testing laboratories or housing societies, offshore structures, bridges, tunnels, highways, transit systems, dams, airports, harbors, water supply and wastewater treatment plants.
