About Department
The post graduate program in VLSI Design with an intake of 18 was started in the year 2008. Very Large Scale Integrated (VLSI) Circuits is a thrust area in modern technological world that has revolutionized the developments in Space technology, Defense, Communication, Instrumentation, Medical Electronics, Computing, Internet, and Modern automations in various industries. VLSI has attained maturity over last few decades during which the technology has shrunk from 10 micrometer to few nanometers by faithfully obeying Mooreâs Law which states that âthe technology shrinks to 1/4th the area in every 18 monthsâ. Thus the designer needs to put four times the circuitry in the same area of chip built 18 months back. This has posed challenges to the designers throughout the world and hence created into a systematic area of study.

Scope & Job Oppourtunities
Having a look at the career prospects, there is bound to be a huge demand for competent engineers in electronic industry to cope the demand in technology. The engineers are involved in sustaining cutting edge technology to stay ahead in competition.
An electronic engineer can find a job in Consumer electronics manufacturing organization, Telecommunication & IT industries, Health care equipment manufacturing, Mobile communication (2G,3G,4G,5G), Internet technologies, Power Electronics, and other industries like steel, petroleum and chemical industry, directing control and testing production process. They may also work with computers and electronic equipment in the medical, manufacturing, industrial control, telecommunications, aeronautical and military fields.
Value Added Course
Laboratories
The VLSI laboratory is well equipped with the state-of-the-art industry standard EDA tools and hardware resources such as Xilinx 9.2i, Xilinx 14.3, Mentor Graphics HEP1, Spartan-3, Spartan-6, Altera Cyclone-2 and Virtex-5 FPGA. The objective of VLSI laboratory is to expose the UG, PG and Research Scholars to understand the concepts of Analogue and Digital IC Design and manufacturing process using the prescribed tools. The VLSI laboratory provides hands-on experience to students in the field of analog and digital circuit IC design to promote the research culture among the young budding Engineers.

Clubs and Activities
The prime aim of this club is not only to provide a strong emphasis in Practical Electronics but also to provide fun in learning and to help students understand the seemingly incomprehensible electronic gadgets in the world today. It also assists students in developing their own devices in an innovative way.
Objectives:
To bring the students out of their syllabi-bound shells and expose them pragmatically to the field.
To explain the concepts of electronics, circuit design, and hardware analysis and help them gain knowledge.
To provide a platform for students to work on their innovative ideas.
To provide resources and the technical know-how to realize and shape their projects.
To expose students to the latest technological advancements in electronics.
To organize discussions on emerging trends in electronics.
